## WORKFLOW: Create Sponsorship Opportunities on a PRF
KEYWORDS: add sponsorship, sponsorships tab, add new, sale price, quantity
JIRA:
---
WHAT: Adds one or more sellable sponsorship opportunities to an existing promotion (PRF) so Sales can market and sell them.
WHO: Promotions Director, Program Director, or General Manager ability.
WHERE: Select the PRF from the Calendar, open the details popover, and go to the Sponsorships tab.
HOW:
1. Open the PRF Details and click the Sponsorships tab.
2. Click the green +Add New button.
3. Fill out the sponsorship form: a name/title (e.g., "Booth Sponsor"), the sale price, a description of what the client receives (e.g., 40 live and 40 recorded mentions), and the quantity of sponsorships available for sale.
4. Click Save — the created sponsorships appear in the PRF's Sponsorships tab.
5. Manage existing sponsorships with the action buttons to edit, delete, or mark as sold; use the upload feature to attach files such as client logos (an icon appears after upload allowing view, download, or delete).
CONSTRAINTS: Creating sponsorships requires Promotions Director, Program Director, or General Manager ability — salespeople sell them but do not create the opportunities.
GOTCHAS: Bulk Actions on the sponsorship list allow deleting multiple entries at once as well as selling multiple sponsorships to a single client.
TEST-DATA HINTS: Sponsorship title "Booth Sponsor", sale price $150, description "40 live and 40 recorded mentions", quantity for sale.

## WORKFLOW: Create a Recurring (Monthly/Weekly/Daily) Sponsorship
KEYWORDS: recurring sponsorship, interval, select days, start date, end date
JIRA:
---
WHAT: Generates a repeating series of sponsorship opportunities across a promotion's run, such as a weekly sponsor for an on-air feature.
WHO: Promotions Director, Program Director, or General Manager ability.
WHERE: PRF Details > Sponsorships tab > green +Add New button.
HOW:
1. Open the PRF Details of the promotion and click the Sponsorships tab.
2. Click the green +Add New button.
3. Enter the Title of the sponsorship, how much it will cost, the details, and the quantity per recurrence (per week, for example — usually 1).
4. Check the Recurring checkbox.
5. Enter the Start Date and End Date, choose the Interval (e.g., weekly), Select Days (e.g., Monday through Friday), and select a Start Day and End Day if required.
6. Click Save — the recurring sponsorships appear scheduled out on the Sponsorships tab through the end of the specified period.
CONSTRAINTS: The recurrence range should align with the PRF's own start and end dates.
GOTCHAS: Quantity is per recurrence, not total — entering 1 with a weekly interval over a month yields roughly four separate sellable opportunities, not one.
TEST-DATA HINTS: Weekly price $150, Interval "weekly", Days Monday–Friday, for a noon music hour feature.

## WORKFLOW: Sell a Sponsorship (Sold It) and Export Available Inventory
KEYWORDS: sold it, sponsorships tab, client name, sale price, export sponsorships
JIRA:
---
WHAT: Marks a sponsorship opportunity as sold to a client and captures the sale details; also exports remaining available inventory for client pitches.
WHO: Sales team members (salespeople) working from the main Calendar's Sponsorships tab.
WHERE: Main Calendar > Sponsorships tab (shows all future sponsorships until they are in the past), or the PRF's Details > Sponsorships tab.
HOW:
1. Locate the sponsorship on the Sponsorships tab and click "Sold It."
2. Enter the client's name, record the sale price, add the salesperson's name, and enter any necessary notes.
3. Save — the status updates to sold and the item turns gray to prevent duplicate sales.
4. To share inventory with a client, export the list of available upcoming sponsorships to an Excel or PDF document for any station.
CONSTRAINTS: The Sponsorships tab only displays future sponsorships; past-dated opportunities drop off the list.
GOTCHAS: The gray-out after selling is the visual guard against double-selling the same inventory — if an item still appears white/active, the sale was not saved.
TEST-DATA HINTS: Sale price $150, client name, salesperson name.

## WORKFLOW: Sell Multiple Sponsorships at Once (Bulk Sell)
KEYWORDS: sell multiple, bulk actions, shift select, multiple sponsorships
JIRA:
---
WHAT: Sells several sponsorships for the same PRF or Feature to a single client using one form and one approval.
WHO: Salespeople with sponsorship-selling access.
WHERE: Sponsorships tab > checkboxes to the left of each sponsorship > Bulk Actions button.
HOW:
1. Check the box to the left of the first sponsorship you want to mark as Sold — the list then narrows to only sponsorships for that same PRF or Feature.
2. Check the boxes for each additional sponsorship going to the same client. For a consecutive run (e.g., consecutive weekly sponsorships), find the last one in the group and hold SHIFT while checking its box to select everything between the first and last.
3. Click the Bulk Actions button and choose "Sell Multiple."
4. Complete the required fields on the single combined form and click "Save."
CONSTRAINTS: All selected sponsorships must belong to the same PRF or Feature and be sold to one single client. After the first selection, the list filters to that PRF/Feature only.
GOTCHAS: The whole group goes live together if no approval process exists, or moves through the sponsorship approval process as a single group — one form, one approval for the batch.
TEST-DATA HINTS: Select a run of consecutive weekly sponsorships via SHIFT-click.

## WORKFLOW: Enable and Add On-Air Feature Segments
KEYWORDS: feature segments, features tab, new feature, firm options, on air tab
JIRA:
---
WHAT: Feature Segments let you schedule recurring on-air features in vPromotions so they can carry sponsorships without needing a PRF, a special liner category, or a scheduled liner.
WHO: A Firm (Market) Admin enables the feature; Promotions Directors, Program Directors, and General Managers add the features.
WHERE: Settings > Firm > Firm Options (enable); the Features tab on the vPromotions Calendar (add/manage).
HOW:
1. A Firm Admin goes to Settings > Firm > Firm Options and, under "General," checks Enable Feature Segments — this adds the "Features" tab to the main calendar. Click Save at the bottom of the Firm Options tab.
2. Under "Sales," also check Use Sold Sponsorship Approvals (recommended when using Feature Sponsorships), which reveals a button for creating an approval process for all Sold Sponsorships. Click Save.
3. From the vPromotions Calendar, click the Features tab to open the Main Features list of scheduled features.
4. Click the green New Feature button and complete the Feature Form for the on-air feature being added.
5. Click View Feature Segments to see the Feature Segments calendar (viewable weekly or monthly), where individual occurrences can be deleted when they won't happen due to special programming, holidays, etc.
CONSTRAINTS: The Features tab does not exist until a Firm Admin enables Feature Segments in Firm Options.
GOTCHAS: Without Sold Sponsorship Approvals in place, feature sponsor copy entered by the seller appears in the On Air tab immediately with no review step — enable approvals if copy should be vetted before it reaches the studio.
TEST-DATA HINTS: n/a (setup workflow).

## WORKFLOW: Create and Sell a Feature Sponsorship
KEYWORDS: feature sponsorship, sponsor copy, sold it, feature sponsorship report
JIRA:
---
WHAT: Attaches sellable sponsorship opportunities directly to a scheduled on-air Feature, bypassing the PRF and liner-scheduling process entirely.
WHO: Promotions/Program Directors create the opportunities; salespeople sell them.
WHERE: Sponsorship tab > +New Sponsorship button, with the "Feature Sponsorship" checkbox; results appear in the On Air tab.
HOW:
1. Schedule the on-air features first in the Features tab.
2. Go to the Sponsorship tab and click the +New Sponsorship button.
3. Check the "Feature Sponsorship" box — the "Feature" selector replaces the PRF selector, letting you assign the sponsorship directly to the on-air Feature.
4. Complete the sponsorship details and Save.
5. To sell, click "Sold It" as with a promotion sponsorship, and complete the required Sponsor Copy field with the copy to be read on air.
6. To report on aired feature sponsorships, go to Reports and run the Feature Sponsorship Report: choose Stations, enter the Client name, and set the Start and End Dates.
CONSTRAINTS: The Sponsor Copy field is required when selling a Feature Sponsorship. Only sponsorships Marked as Read or Tracked appear in the Feature Sponsorship Report.
GOTCHAS: Scheduled features appear in the On Air Tab shaded in yellow at their scheduled times; three green dollar signs ($$$) appear to the right of the title when a sponsorship has been sold for that feature. Approved sponsor copy flows straight into the On Air tab.
TEST-DATA HINTS: Client name and date range for the Feature Sponsorship Report.

## WORKFLOW: Add a Digital or Off-Air Feature Sponsorship
KEYWORDS: digital feature, off-air feature, client url, new feature checkbox
JIRA:
---
WHAT: Creates sponsorship opportunities for website pages, app features, or anything that should not appear in the on-air studio.
WHO: Users with access to create Features (Promotions Director, Program Director, General Manager).
WHERE: Features tab > green New Feature button > Digital/Off-Air Feature checkbox.
HOW:
1. Click the Features tab.
2. Click the green New Feature button.
3. Check the Digital/Off-Air Feature checkbox.
4. Fill out all required fields.
5. Click Save.
CONSTRAINTS: Digital/Off-Air Features do not recur at daily times and days of the week — they use a single start date and end date for the overall feature. A client URL is required when "Sold It" is clicked.
GOTCHAS: These features intentionally do not show in the on-air studio, so don't expect them in the On Air tab alongside standard yellow-shaded feature segments.
TEST-DATA HINTS: Client URL required at sale time (e.g., a station website sponsor landing page).

## WORKFLOW: Create and Sell a Prize Sponsorship for a Contest
KEYWORDS: prize sponsorship, require prizes, prizes provided, contest sponsor
JIRA:
---
WHAT: A sponsorship variant where the client is required to supply the prize for a contest or giveaway.
WHO: Promotions/Program Directors create it; salespeople sell it.
WHERE: PRF Details > Sponsorships tab (creation), then the main Sponsorships tab or Details > Sponsorships tab (selling).
HOW:
1. Create the sponsorship as usual but check the "Require Prizes" checkbox — this is the only difference from a standard sponsorship.
2. For a recurring arrangement (e.g., a month-long contest with weekly Monday–Friday sponsorships where the client provides a prize each week), configure the recurrence accordingly.
3. The Promotions Director or Program Director adds the provided prize to that week's plays via Details > Plays for the promotion.
4. When sold, the salesperson marks it Sold from the main Sponsorships tab or the Details > Sponsorships tab; the sale form is the same as other sponsorships except a prize is required in the Prizes Provided field.
CONSTRAINTS: The Prizes Provided field is mandatory at sale time for prize sponsorships — the sale cannot be completed without it.
GOTCHAS: The prize supplied by the sponsor still needs to be attached to the correct week's plays manually in Details > Plays; checking "Require Prizes" alone does not schedule the prize.
TEST-DATA HINTS: Month-long contest with weekly (Mon–Fri) prize sponsorships.

## WORKFLOW: Enable Sold Sponsorship Approvals
KEYWORDS: sold sponsorship approvals, firm options, pending approval, approval process
JIRA:
---
WHAT: Requires sold sponsorships to be approved before they are finalized and their copy reaches the studio.
WHO: Firm Admin (to enable and configure); designated approvers thereafter.
WHERE: Settings > Firm > Firm Options, under the Sales section.
HOW:
1. Go to Settings > Firm > Firm Options.
2. Under Sales, check the Use Sold Sponsorship Approvals checkbox — this reveals a button for creating an approval process for all Sold Sponsorships.
3. Build the approval process (keep it as simple as possible).
4. Click Save at the bottom of the Firm Options tab.
CONSTRAINTS: The approval process requires at least one person to approve the sponsorship before it is marked as sold; until then the status remains Pending Approval.
GOTCHAS: This setting is especially important with Feature Sponsorships — without it, seller-entered sponsor copy appears in the On Air tab immediately. With it enabled, sponsor copy will not show in the studio unless the sponsorship is approved.
TEST-DATA HINTS: n/a (firm-level configuration).

## WORKFLOW: Configure Sponsorship Email Notifications
KEYWORDS: sponsorship notifications, email settings, days before sponsorship start, firm options
JIRA:
---
WHAT: Email alerts for sponsorship lifecycle events (sold, cancelled, created, file uploaded) and advance reminders before a sponsorship starts or ends.
WHO: Firm Admins configure seller reminders at the firm level; individual managers (Sales Manager, Program Director, Promotions Director, General Manager abilities) configure their own manager reminders; any user can set lifecycle-change notifications.
WHERE: Settings > Firm > Firm Options (Sales section) for seller reminders; Settings > User > Email Settings for manager reminders and lifecycle notifications.
HOW:
1. Firm-level (for sellers): In Settings > Firm > Firm Options under Sales, select "Remind Sponsorship Sales Person Before Sponsorship Start" and enter a value in the "Days before Sponsorship Start" field that appears; and/or select "Remind Sponsorship Sales Person Before Sponsorship End" and enter a value in "Days before Sponsorship End."
2. Manager-level: In Settings > User > Email Settings, select "Email me _ days before a sponsorship starts" and/or "Email me _ days before a sponsorship ends," entering the desired number of days in the field that opens.
3. Lifecycle notifications: In Settings > User > Email Settings, enable email notification for when a Promotional Sponsorship is sold, cancelled, created, or when a new file is uploaded to a Sponsorship.
CONSTRAINTS: Only the salesperson who sold the sponsorship receives the firm-level seller reminder emails. Manager start/end reminder options are limited to users with Sales Manager, Program Director, Promotions Director, or General Manager abilities.
GOTCHAS: Firm-level seller reminders and user-level manager reminders are separate settings — enabling one does not enable the other, so both must be configured if sellers and managers should both be notified.
TEST-DATA HINTS: "Days before Sponsorship Start" = 7, "Days before Sponsorship End" = 14.
